Armario privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-08

What Armario collects

Nothing. Armario has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting, and the app does not ask for the internet permission. We do not receive your photos, your pieces, your outfits or anything else you do in the app.

Where your things live

Your pieces, photos and outfits are stored on your phone and nowhere else. If you delete the app, they are gone from the phone unless you made a backup.

Camera

The camera is used only to photograph the pieces you add. Photos are not sent anywhere.

Your photo library

When you choose photos from your library, Armario uses the system photo picker, so the app gets only the photos you pick and has no permission to browse your library.

Cutting out a piece

On iPhone, Apple's Vision framework cuts the piece out on the phone. On Android, Google Play services delivers a cut-out model (Google ML Kit) that runs on the phone; Google Play services is a separate Google component and may report usage under Google's own privacy policy. Armario itself sends nothing.

Backups

A backup is a file you create and keep. Armario saves or shares it only where you choose to send it, and it restores a backup only from a file you pick.

Feedback

If you write to us, we use your email address only to reply to you.

Contact

Questions about this page? Write to armario.app.feedback@gmail.com, or use Send feedback in the app's About screen.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page and its effective date changes. A change will not make Armario collect data without a new version of this page saying so first.
